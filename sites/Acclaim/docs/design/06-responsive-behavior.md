# 06 — Responsive behavior

## Established direction

- The experience always fits within one viewport and does not page-scroll.
- The navbar remains above the map on desktop and mobile.
- The map scales to the available canvas.
- State abbreviations remain the primary compact label format.
- External callouts are allowed where small states cannot contain their labels.
- Touch targets must remain usable even when the geographic shape is small.

## Open

- Final mobile navbar contents and arrangement.
- Whether some small-state callouts should use a dedicated mobile cluster.
- Minimum supported viewport dimensions.
