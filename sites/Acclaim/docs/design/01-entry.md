# 01 — Entry

## Purpose

Present a quiet, singular invitation into the experience.

## Visible state

- The viewport is completely black.
- `Actions` is the only visible element.
- The word is white, centered horizontally and vertically, and uses the current serif typeface.
- No hint, icon, border, navigation, map, or additional decoration is visible.
- The page does not scroll.

## Interaction

- Hovering or keyboard-focusing `Actions` gently enlarges the word.
- Activating it once locks the word at its hover size and begins the reveal transition.
- The word never grows beyond its hover size.
- Further activation is ignored while the reveal is running.

## Acceptance

- Only `Actions` is visually present before activation.
- The control works with pointer, Enter, and Space.
- The hover treatment does not change the word from white.
